How it works

  1. Triage traverses the dependency graph, runs PageRank/betweenness and correlates deploys to find the root cause and blast radius.
  2. Historian runs in parallel. It recalls past incidents, runbooks, ADRs and facts learned in earlier sessions.
  3. Commander reads both agents' findings from the graph, picks who to page, and records a Decision linked to its evidence.
  4. Resolve it, and the investigation becomes an Incident in memory. The next session will find it.
The decision's evidence chain will appear here: Decision → Findings → graph nodes.
Every graph query the agents run is logged here.
